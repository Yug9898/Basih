# Basih downloads

Mac release **1.0002.00.34** supports **M1-or-later Macs running macOS 26 or later**. The download contains the native My Basih app. Intel Macs, older macOS versions, the legacy app and Windows are not supported by this release.

[Download Basih for Mac](https://github.com/Yug9898/Basih/raw/refs/heads/main/Basih-for-Friend.zip)

Unzip and double-click **Install Basih.command**. The installer checks your Mac and macOS before replacing an app. Existing local entries, People balances, backups and Keychain credentials stay on your Mac. Existing supported installations can upgrade to My Basih.

This update:

- Create or sign in to your online Basih account from People. Your existing local People entries, ledger entries and balances remain unchanged during account setup.
- Connect with another person, accept requests and manage linked entries from People. Requests remain on the server until the recipient signs in.
- Your online account ID is separate from your local ledger. Only actions you choose to share through a connection are shared.
- Preserves the current financial integrity, entry recovery, backup and native startup fixes.

To restore a backup, open **Settings → Backups → Choose backup…**, select the SQLite backup and confirm its passcode. A safety backup is created first; the selected original is retained. A backup moved to another Mac may require reconnecting bank or merchant-search credentials.

These apps are ad-hoc signed, not notarized. macOS may require approval in Privacy & Security on first launch. Touch ID may be unavailable without distribution provisioning; passcode unlock is supported.

Downloads contain no personal ledgers, backups or API keys. Older artifacts remaining in this repository are historical downloads, not supported current releases.
